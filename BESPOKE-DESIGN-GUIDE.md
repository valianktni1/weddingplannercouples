# Bespoke stationery designer workflow

IVORY DIGITAL — EDITABLE BESPOKE DESIGN PACK

This is a designer/admin format. Couples only see watermarked proofs and their paid output. No live AI account or API is used.

Edit manifest.json, keeping format ivory-bespoke-1 and all nine items. Add original PNG/JPEG/WebP artwork files alongside it in the ZIP, with ordinary filenames and no folders. Set each item background to the matching filename, or blank for a typographic design. Never flatten guest names or table assignments into backgrounds.

Each item has size (one of its existing planner sizes), background_colour, art_bleed_mm (0 or 3), approved content and fields. Text boxes x,y,w,h are fractions 0..1 of the printed face. For folded place cards, the printed face is the lower half. Font size and minimum size are fractions of page width; the renderer enforces a 9pt floor and blocks overflow. Font: serif, italic, sans. Alignment: center or left. Colours: six-digit hex. Fields can be names,date,intro,message,venue,arrival,closing,menu,recipient,table_name,seating or static. Static headings use text. Names/date/menu/wording come from approved content. Recipient, tables and seating use current confirmed guests. Place cards, table cards and charts must retain their corresponding live field.

Art with art_bleed_mm=3 must cover the full paper plus 3mm on each edge. Use suitable image resolution: aim for 300dpi on small pieces and at least 150dpi on large signs. Text remains embedded vector text. Check every item, font character and actual-size test print; do not rely only on low-resolution proofs. Physical printing, paper, colour conversion and finishing are arranged by the couple/printer.

Upload a revised pack for the one combined change request. Publishing any new pack invalidates approval and locks clean downloads until the couple approves it, including corrections after payment. Payment remains recorded; correcting your own error does not charge the couple again. Old pack assets remain private for recovery.

For each brief: accept the agreed quote, download the starter, prepare original artwork and all nine layouts, upload the complete ZIP and inspect every proof. Send a reviewed update to the couple. Process their one combined revision if needed, then request payment after approval. Verify the actual GBP receipt before unlocking. Check final actual-size output independently before advising the couple to print.
