# Stationery asset provenance

Botanical corner and meadow border: original artwork generated with the built-in image tool on 24 October 2026 for this project. These are separate artwork assets, not flattened invitation text. PNG originals remain in the generation workspace. The project copies are RGBA WebP exports; neither contains couple data. No competitor artwork was copied.

Botanical prompt: Original delicate botanical corner illustration on transparency: olive/eucalyptus leaves in muted sage and deep green, pale cream buds, fine natural stems, airy triangular corner, no text/paper/card/shadow/border.

Meadow prompt: Original low wildflower border on transparency: dusty pink cosmos, soft blue cornflowers, ivory chamomile, fine sage stems, varied heights, open centre, no text/paper/card/shadow/frame.

Fonts: Cormorant Garamond and Outfit, from the official Google Fonts repository, instantiated locally at weight 400 with fontTools. Included SIL Open Font Licences retain the original copyright notices. Runtime needs no fontTools and uses the included TTFs for PDF embedding and PNG rendering.

Sources:
- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond
- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/outfit

Collection previews are rendered privately from the signed-in couple's current details. No example couple photographs are bundled. Images, font data and rendering code are served locally; there are no runtime AI calls.

Visual correction, 24 October 2026: `meadow-frame.webp` is a new original transparent portrait frame generated with the built-in image tool. Prompt: airy hand-painted watercolour wildflowers growing from the bottom and climbing the edges, dusty pink cosmos, cornflower blue, ivory daisies and sage foliage; generous transparent centre, no text, paper, shadows or mockup. The separate editable text is rendered by the app. The smaller place-card sprig is cropped from the original meadow artwork at runtime.

`CormorantGaramond-Italic.ttf` is the weight-400 static instance of the official Google Fonts Cormorant Garamond italic variable font, under the existing included Cormorant Garamond SIL Open Font Licence. Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond/CormorantGaramond-Italic%5Bwght%5D.ttf
